Errata: what you get, and what you don’t

You get

  • A new site, hosted for you, with checkout, email, ads and content.
  • A co-founder that explains what it does and why.
  • Every file of your business, readable and yours.

You don’t get

  • Stock, sourcing and shipping. You bring the supplier, or sell digital products and services.
  • Edits to the site you have. It reads it for context and builds a new one here.
  • Sales made up to look good. Only completed sales count.
  • Live money, ads, posts or email on day one. Each waits for your own provider account, and says so.
