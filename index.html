What your co-founder does

One conversation, and every part of the business beside it. Big decisions come to you for approval; with autopilot on, the rest just happens.

Builds and ships the site

A storefront on its own address, a brand kit, a logo, and every change tested before it goes live.

Takes payment

Products and subscriptions, checkout, orders and customers. Try a test buy before your first real one.

Runs the inbox

A business email address. Your co-founder drafts and answers; you stay in control of every send.

Makes the content

Offer images, short videos and posts in your brand, with a range of credits shown before anything is made.

Finds customers

A launch week plan, a warm list, outbound sequences, social posting and one careful ad campaign inside the budget you set.

Remembers everything

The business lives in plain files you can read: its soul, its owner, its playbook, its memory and the map of its code.

How it works

  1. Say the idea. One sentence is enough. Name the business and your co-founder.
  2. Day one. Your co-founder drafts the brand, the site, a first product and checkout, and asks you before anything big.
  3. Launch week. Seven days, one step a day: your warm list, personal notes, launch posts, launch day, follow-ups, a debrief.
  4. Every morning. A briefing in chat and by email: visitors, carts, sales, what is waiting on you, and today's plan.

What you get, and what you don't

You get

  • A new site, hosted for you, with checkout, email, ads and content.
  • A co-founder that explains what it does and why.
  • Every file of your business, readable and yours.

You don't get

  • Sourcing, stock or shipping of physical goods: you bring the supplier, or sell digital products and services.
  • Edits to a site you already have. Your co-founder can read it for context and builds a new one here if you ask.
  • Sales made up to look good: only completed sales are counted.
