What your co-founder does

And where each part stops until you connect your own account. It never pretends.

Site

A storefront on its own address, built and deployed from files you can read. Your own domain on paid plans.

Stops at: your registrar’s DNS record.

Payments

Products with one-time or recurring prices, and a checkout you can test with a test card.

Stops at: your payment provider’s keys.

Email

A business address from day one. It drafts replies; you approve every send.

Stops at: nothing. It works.

Content

Offer images, short clips and posts in your brand, with the credits shown before anything is made.

Stops at: image models not configured on a stack.

Customers

A warm list, personal notes and outbound to leads you upload. Only the people you give.

Stops at: a send without your approval.

Ads

One careful campaign inside a budget you approve, with a readiness checklist that blocks launch.

Stops at: your ad account.

Memory

Plain files you can open: its soul, the business, the owner, the playbook, what it learned.

Stops at: nothing. They are your files.

Routines

A daily briefing with the real numbers, and routines you write in plain words: pause, edit, run now.

Stops at: nothing. They run on schedule.

What you get, and what you don’t

You get

  • A new site, hosted for you, with checkout, email, content and ads.
  • A co-founder that explains what it does and why, and asks before it spends.
  • Every file of your business, readable and yours.
  • A CommandAGI account underneath: the same agents, files and sites, under this brand.

You don’t get

  • Stock, sourcing and shipping. You bring the supplier, or sell digital products and services.
  • Edits to a site you already have. It reads it for context and builds a new one here.
  • Sales made up to look good. Only completed sales count. A test order is a test.
  • Live money, ads, posts or outbound mail on day one. Each waits for your own provider account, and says so.

Questions

Is the co-founder a person?

No. It is an AI agent that runs on CommandAGI, and it says so to you and to anyone it writes to. It has tools over your files, your tasks, your routines and your storefront, and nothing else.

What does it do without asking?

With autopilot off, it proposes and waits. With autopilot on, it does routine work inside the limits you set and reports what it did. Spend, public posts, changes to the live site, contact lists and legal filings always wait for you.

Can I take real money on day one?

The checkout runs in test mode until you add your payment provider’s keys in Settings. A test order is marked as a test everywhere and never counts as a sale.

Who owns the files?

You do. A business is a folder in your CommandAGI Drive: the record, the co-founder’s memory, products, orders, leads, tasks. You can open every file.

What is VibeStartup, legally?

A brand of CommandAGI Inc, a Delaware C corporation. Your account is a CommandAGI account; CommandAGI’s terms and privacy policy apply.

What does it cost?

Prices are set at launch. Until then you start with free credits, and every plan page says “set at launch”.
